ProofChain independent verifier

Verify a document

This page checks a document against a published Merkle root. Everything is computed here in your browser: it sends your files nowhere, uses no ProofChain code or server, and never contacts the registry.

A pass means the document is identical to one registered at that point in the ledger. It does not prove who created the document or that its contents are true. Without a pinned checkpoint, a ledger that was rewritten in full and signed again would not be noticed.
