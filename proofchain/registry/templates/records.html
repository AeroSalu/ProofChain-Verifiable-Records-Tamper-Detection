Records and batches

Each custodian has their own open batch, and a record joins the batch of whoever confirms it. Sealing a batch builds its Merkle tree and publishes the root to the ledger. After that the batch cannot change, and each record has a proof to download. A batch is sealed by its owner{% if is_admin %}; as an admin you can seal any open batch{% else %} or by an admin{% endif %}.
