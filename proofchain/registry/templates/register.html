The Canonical Hashing Engine

01
Canonical Reduction

"A hash covers bytes, not meaning." Documents are reduced to exact canonical byte form (json-sorted-v1 or raw-bytes-v1).

02
SHA-256 Fingerprint

Computed over canonical bytes. Regardless of key order, formatting, or platform, equivalent records yield identical 64-char hex strings.

03
RFC 6962 Domain Separation

Leaves are prefixed with 0x00 (SHA-256(0x00 || fp)) and branch nodes with 0x01, preventing leaf-node confusion attacks (CVE-2012-2459).

Float Rejection Notice

Per RFC 8785 specification, json-sorted-v1 strictly forbids IEEE-754 floating point numbers to avoid cross-language rounding discrepancies.
